# Drafts & Locking

## 1. Edit sessions

When you click **Edit**, the wiki creates a lock on the page. Other users see the page as locked and cannot edit it simultaneously.

## 1. Auto-save

While editing, your changes are automatically saved as a draft at regular intervals (default: every 2 minutes, configurable in Admin > Configuration). Drafts are private — other users see the published version.

## 1. Publishing

Click **Publish** to save your changes as a new version. This releases the lock and makes your edits visible to everyone.

## 1. Discarding

Click **Discard** to throw away your draft and release the lock. The page reverts to the last published version.

## 1. Stale locks

If you forget to publish or discard, the lock expires after a configurable timeout (default: 24 hours). Admins can force-discard locks from the Admin > Locks tab.

## 1. Draft recovery

If your browser crashes while editing, your draft is preserved. When you return to the page and click Edit, the draft is restored automatically.
